Rules used

  • Before round 1: every archer on both sides rolls once (11–20 = 1 kill). An outpost fires 1 shot and a capital fires 2 at the attacker (11–20 = 1 kill each). These all happen at the same time.
  • Each round: each side rolls one D20. 1–5 = 0 kills, 6–15 = 1 kill, 16–20 = 2 kills (only 1 kill if that side is down to its last unit). Both sides' kills land at the same time.
  • Casualties: infantry die first, then cavalry, then archers.
  • Cavalry: each cavalry death rolls 14–20 (35%) to dismount into a new infantry. That infantry is next in line, so a second kill from the same roll hits it.
  • Kill experience counts every unit removed, including cavalry that dismount.
  • Not modelled: battles with 3+ players, the 12/24 unit caps on dismounted infantry, and what happens after (evictions, sending extra units home).
